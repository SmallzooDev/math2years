e-24.

Let $a, b$ be integers. Define $a \equiv b \pmod{5}$, which we read "$a$ is congruent to $b$ modulo $5$", to mean that $a - b$ is divisible by $5$.

Prove: If $a \equiv b \pmod{5}$ and $x \equiv y \pmod{5}$, then

$$
\begin{aligned}
a + x &\equiv b + y \pmod{5}, \\
ax &\equiv by \pmod{5}.
\end{aligned}
$$
